HobbySwap Privacy Policy

HobbySwap Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

HobbySwap ("the app") is a marketplace where people swap or sell hobby gear and skills. This policy explains what we collect and how it's used.

Information we collect

  • Account information (only if you create an account; you can browse without one): your email address, a password (stored securely by our account provider, never in plain text), and the display name you choose.
  • Content you post: listings (title, description, category, location text, value, optional price, and photos), "looking for" posts, swap offers, buy requests, chat messages, meetups you host (title, public meeting place, date and time), meetup RSVPs, meetup details agreed in chat, and ratings you give.
  • Saved items and activity: listings you save, and view counts on listings.
  • Safety information: reports you file (including why) and users you block.
  • Basic usage events: simple actions such as opening the app, posting a listing, or sending an offer, along with a random device ID (not an advertising ID) so we can count unique devices and understand how the app is used. These are linked to your account when you're signed in.
  • Feedback you send: the type, your message, an optional contact email, and your account if you're signed in. Feedback is visible only to the HobbySwap team.
  • Device location, if you allow it: used on your device to show nearby listings and filter by distance. Your precise location is not sent to our servers or shared with other users. When you post a listing, the city and state you enter (and map coordinates derived from them) are stored with that listing and visible to others.
  • Photos: only photos you choose to add to a listing. The app doesn't access your photo library otherwise.

How information is used

To run the marketplace: show your listings and offers to other users, deliver messages, display ratings, keep the community safe (reviewing reports and removing content or accounts that break our Terms), and let you sign in. We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see what

  • Your display name, listings, photos, "looking for" posts, and listing city/state are visible to anyone who opens the app, including visitors who haven't created an account.
  • Offers and chat messages are visible only to the two people involved.
  • Meetups you host (title, place, date and time, your display name, and how many people are going) are visible to everyone. Which meetups you've joined is visible only to you.
  • Your email address is not shown to other users.

Where data is stored

Data is stored with our infrastructure provider, Supabase (supabase.com), which hosts the database, file storage, and sign-in for the app.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, listings, photos, offers, and messages. You can also remove individual listings, block other users, and revoke location or photo access in your device settings.

Children

HobbySwap is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Contact

Questions or requests: hobbyswapinfo@gmail.com

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
